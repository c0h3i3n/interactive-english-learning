Reading Comprehension

The Lemonade Helpers

On Saturday morning, Mia and her friends cleaned their clubhouse. The children wanted to buy new chairs, so they made a lemonade stand. Mia squeezed the lemons. Ben counted the cups, and Sara made a small sign. In the afternoon, many neighbors came by. The children sold twenty-four cups of lemonade. They were tired, but they felt proud because they worked together.

1. Why did the children make a lemonade stand?

2. Who counted the cups?

3. How many cups did they sell?

4. How did the children feel?

5. What did Mia do? Answer with one complete sentence.

6. Why were the children tired? Answer with one complete sentence.

2

3. The neighbors ___ lemonade now.

6. They ___ twenty-four cups yesterday.

Reading Comprehension

A Day on the Snowy Island

Ed lived on a cold island with his friends. One morning, snow covered the road. Ed and Betty wanted to visit the penguins, but they could not walk far. They made a small sled from a box and a strong rope. Ed pulled the sled, and Betty carried warm food. At noon, they reached the penguin house. The penguins were hungry, so the children gave them fish. On the way home, Ed waved to his new friends.

1. What covered the road?

2. What did Ed and Betty make?

3. What did Betty carry?

4. Why did the children give the penguins fish?

5. What did Ed do on the way home? Answer with one complete sentence.

6. How did the children travel? Answer with one complete sentence.

2

2. Ed ___ the sled yesterday.
3. Betty ___ warm food now.
4. The children ___ the penguins last week.
5. Ed ___ to his friends every day.
6. They ___ fish to the penguins.

Reading Comprehension

The Clever Bird

Bird lived near a green forest. She was small, but she remembered everything she saw. One day, Tiger asked Bird to find a quiet place for him to sleep. Bird flew over the forest and saw a dark cave. She did not tell Tiger about the cave. Instead, she made a plan. She sang a loud song near the cave. Tiger followed the song and fell asleep there. Bird flew back to her tree and helped the other animals stay safe.

1. Where did Bird live?

2. What did Tiger ask Bird to find?

3. What did Bird do near the cave?

4. Why did Bird make a plan?

5. What happened to Tiger? Answer with one complete sentence.

6. How did Bird help the other animals? Answer with one complete sentence.

1. Birds ___ in trees.
