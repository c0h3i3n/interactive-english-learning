A Busy Saturday

Last Saturday, Ben went to the park with his family. He rode his bike and played soccer with his sister. His mom watched them under a big tree. His dad took pictures.

Ben saw a small bird near the flowers. The bird could not fly well. Ben and his sister did not touch it. They told their dad. His dad called a worker at the park. The worker helped the bird.

Ben felt happy because he helped an animal. Now Ben goes to the park every Saturday. He likes to ride his bike, play soccer, and look at birds.

1. Where did Ben go last Saturday?

2. Who did Ben play soccer with?

3. What did Ben see near the flowers?

4. Did Ben and his sister touch the bird?

5. Why did Ben feel happy?

6. What does Ben do every Saturday now?

7. What is the best title for this story?

8. Which sentence is true?

Sam's New Job at Home

Sam helps his family every day. He feeds the fish in the morning and puts his toys away at night. His sister waters the plants, and his dad cooks dinner.

After dinner, Sam made a small chart. He wrote, "Feed the fish," on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. Now he checks the chart every morning so he does not forget.
